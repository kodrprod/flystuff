## Big idea
Revision 2. Every listing in the category says «88 клавиш». In-stock prices run from 232 960 ₸ to 754 990 ₸ on the models we need to move. Buyers can't see what the extra money buys, and the old answer, «listen to the difference», does not survive a weak phone mic, platform compression and a phone speaker. So the hero no longer asks viewers to hear anything. It **shows the differences a phone camera captures reliably**:
- key travel when a finger presses and releases;
- what is on the floor (stand, 1 pedal or 3, a bench or none);
- headphones going in, then a silent room;
- the depth and look of the body (cabinet vs portable).

Each claim about a model is shown only once it is in that model's spec card in the ledger.

**Platform idea «Покажем до оплаты».**
- Device, frame 1 of every episode: top-down, two in-stock pianos side by side, one finger pressing the same key on each with the real key thud. On-screen question «[разница] — за что?», with no ₸ figure in the first 2 seconds (G4).
- Fixed end frame: «Астана, Кажымукана 14 · WhatsApp: слово ___».

> Покажем, за что вы платите, — до того, как заплатите.

This is not a forecast. Text judges are near chance on short-form (L001, ρ 0.07 for retail), so this is a better-built set of shots on goal. What changed, and why it is the better bet:

1. **The format can now keep its promise.** The hero shows key travel, pedals, stand, headphones and body depth. A weak phone films these reliably. A 4-second sound A/B through phone mic → compression → phone speaker would mostly get «одинаково» and push viewers to the cheapest listing anywhere. Sound comparison survives only as one exploration episode, and only if it passes a staff blind playback test on a cheap Android and the boss approves after listening.

2. **Reach goes to the stock that has to move.** Week 1 is Kurzweil CUP E1/M1 (3+3 units), week 2 is EDP-220BK (9) vs PX-S1100 (5), week 3 adds AP-300WE (4). Together these are 24 units in the first three weeks of hero content. Nothing promotes a 1-unit 2 M ₸ piano.

3. **We don't talk buyers out of our own Casio stock.** Casio's Almaty-only service stays fully disclosed in the WA reply, if asked, and on the trust highlight in exact site wording. It is no longer a hook line in a weekly Status. The warranty line in videos waits for the owner to confirm how the shop handles repairs.

4. **It uses the local buying lever.** Instalments («от X ₸/мес») are now owner question #1 in week 0. If they are confirmed, they reframe the budget, gift and «а если бросит?» videos. The return-policy angle that helped nobody is gone.

5. **Distribution is local, not CIS musicians.** A teacher track (ДМШ teachers → class parent chats, coded ПЕДАГОГ1/РОДИТЕЛИ1), forwardable vertical utility cuts, and the Astana address on every end frame point reach at buyers in Astana.

6. **The shoot is realistic.** Each session uses ≤2 pianos, ≤3 setups and ≤10 minutes, with a preset/volume/framing checklist taped to each piano. That is four small October sessions instead of one impossible 17-minute marathon.

7. **It fixes our known failure mode.** Demos are tied to a buyer's money question (L006), there are no price slides (L005), and a YouTube model-name search line reuses the footage (L002/L004) at zero extra minutes.

## Measurement and decisions
- Metric: Business: digital pianos sold and attributed by 2026-12-31.

The target is assumed at +10 attributed sales ≈ 50 coded inquiries (assumed 20% inquiry-to-order rate). It is replaced by the owner's Q2 number and the chat-sample rate.

Thompson reward: E-score = √(rel_reach × rel_actions) at T+7d, scored as beating the median.
- YouTube is normalised against the channel's own median.
- Instagram and TikTok use the pooled music-retail median, a hypothesis until there are 10 posts per platform.
- The reward switches to coded piano WA inquiries per 1k reach at ≥10 coded inquiries in a rolling 4 weeks.

Tail: ≥1 post at ≥5× the median by 20 Dec (likely, not guaranteed).
- Attribution: 1. One WA code per video and per platform variant: ВЫБОР1, ПРОВЕРКА1, ПЕДАГОГ1, ПЕРВЫЙ1, СЕМЬЯ1, РАЗНИЦА1, БЮДЖЕТ1, ЗАЧТО1, ПОДАРОК1, with new codes per episode. The teacher/parent card has its own code, РОДИТЕЛИ1, so the local track is measured separately.
2. The WA logger records code → model → city → instalment asked Y/N → sold Y/N, weekly (daily in the push).
3. Weak signals: promoted vs unpromoted piano SKU stock_qty on the site (difference-in-differences), plus Kaspi SKU lift if a Kaspi shop is confirmed.
4. Uncoded inquiries are logged «без кода» and never claimed.
- Review: - Per post: T+48h (R1) and T+7d (reward).
- Monday: posteriors, R4 allocation, TTL re-fetch, the shoot card (≤10 min, ≤2 pianos).

- Checkpoints: 2026-11-02 recalibration; 2026-11-22 for ≥10 coded inquiries, SMP lock and R8; 2026-12-20 switch to the push mix; 2026-12-31 final attribution.
- Rule: Mix: 50/35/15 (hero / proof+utility / conversion) to 20 Dec with exploration ≥25%; 30/30/40 on 21–31 Dec with exploration ≥15%.
- Rule: SKU priority rule. Hero and utility slots go to SKUs with ≥3 units on the site, re-read weekly: EDP-220BK, PX-S1100, AP-300WE, CUP E1/M1, and PX-770/AP-270 on the YouTube line. A 1-unit SKU never gets a hero slot. If a priority SKU drops below 2, its slots move to the next most-stocked in-stock model.
- Rule: Show-not-hear rule. Hero episodes argue only with visible, ledger-backed differences. A sound A/B may run only as one exploration slot, and only if two staff told the pianos apart on a cheap Android in the week-0 test and the boss approved after listening. Line-out recording is used only with a cable the shop already has. A 10× sound comparison never runs.
- Rule: G4 tightening (my call, beyond the slate): no ₸ figure in the first 2 seconds of any video. Prices and gaps appear from 2 s onward, inside the story.
- Rule: Casio service disclosure rule. Full disclosure (2-year warranty, service in Almaty) goes in the WA reply when asked and on the trust highlight in exact site wording. It is never in a hook or a recurring Status. In videos the warranty line is «гарантия 2 года, по гарантийному случаю — [как магазин организует ремонт]», published only after the owner confirms the procedure. It is never hidden.
- Rule: Instalment gate (Q1). If instalments are confirmed, «от X ₸/мес» goes into TIERS-07, GIFT-05 and FIRSTYEAR-15 with the provider and exact terms, owner sign-off on every post, and no «0%» unless confirmed. If they are not confirmed, no monthly figures appear anywhere, and FIRSTYEAR-15 runs the entry-tier version.

- Rule: Shoot rule. Each weekly session uses ≤2 pianos and ≤3 setups and takes ≤10 minutes, with the printed preset/volume/framing checklist taped to each piano. A shot that is skipped is replaced by reuse, never by AI depicting the product.
- Rule: R1 Rescue at T+48h: rel_hold3 < 0.7 → re-cut with the next hook variant and repost ≥72 h later, once. If hold is fine but completion < 0.7, bring the payoff forward next time.
- Rule: R2 Iterate on any post at ≥2× the median: a new pair on the same device, the same insight on a new engine, and an E19 reply.
- Rule: R3 Scale on a post at ≥3× the median with ≥1 code: pin it, add it to Status and the teacher card. Paid only if approved.
- Rule: R4 Allocate every Monday across A1 hero, A2 utility, A3 parent/teacher question and A4 gift (from 1 Dec) by 1 000 Thompson draws. Exploration ≥25%, cap 50% per arm, ≥1 slot every 2 weeks until 6 posts.
- Rule: R5 Retire an arm if P(success rate > 0.5) < 0.15 after ≥8 posts.
- Rule: R6 Report honestly: P(beats median) plus an 80% interval, «пока похоже, что…».
- Rule: R7 Funnel check every 2 weeks: if E is rising but codes are flat, fix the CTA, the codes, reply time, the pinned video and the instalment info before making more content.
- Rule: R8 Rethink: no arm at P > 0.6 and fewer than 10 codes by 22 Nov → rerun C with the chats and micro-voice, and propose a non-discount offer.
- Rule: R9 Stock: re-check on publish day. A promoted SKU at 0 has its posts pulled and swapped. Never promote out-of-stock SKUs.
- Rule: Truth gates: every price or gap literal is re-fetched within 7 days before publishing. Spec claims are made only from fetched spec cards. CHECK-03's generic checks are worded and approved by the store.
