## Big idea
Online, every instrument is silent and every spec sheet reads like code. A first-time buyer can't hear the difference between a 22 100 ₸ ukulele and a 64 501 ₸ one, and can't see what a digital piano box is missing. Muzzone lets them hear and see real, in-stock instruments up close, side by side, with fresh ledger prices. The next step is always a chat: «напишите — подскажем под ваш бюджет и что есть сейчас». Tests: (1) It can run to 20+ episodes: every in-stock beginner pair or trio of ukuleles, guitars, amps, pianos and, if stocked, dombras, plus every recurring buyer question. (2) It is provable on camera, because the sound and the close-up are the proof. Claims are limited to what the ledger or the camera verifies; polyphony was dropped because a phone mic cannot show it. (3) Every episode ends in the measured action, a coded or otherwise attributed inquiry.

> Сначала послушайте вблизи, потом выбирайте

This is evidence plus a learning plan, not a forecast (L001: a text judge predicts retail Shorts at ρ 0.07).
(1) Top tension I01 (strength 4): first-time buyers can't judge sound from specs and fear wasting money. 8/8 of Muzzone's beginner titles got ≥11 000 views, the best 64 000 (E38, E39). We dramatise the tension rather than the phrase «для начинающих», which did not replicate on Shorts (E40, L003).
(2) Close sound is what one weak phone can do, if it is handled honestly: own short guitar-sound clips got 14 000–32 000 views (I02: E42, E43), while brand-only cymbal demos sat at a 390 median (L006). So every clip answers a buyer question. After the creator review, the audio protocol now protects the comparison itself: ukuleles tuned with a clip-on tuner, ID03 shot as one continuous fixed-distance take, phone noise suppression off.
(3) Claims shrink to what is provable. ID05 no longer 'shows' polyphony and uses no AI voice. It runs only on staff-confirmed visible or audible differences, in a staff member's own voice, or it is killed. ID06's checks are now things parents actually get wrong (key weight, headphone outputs, bench and pedals). ID19 drops the unverified Astana courier line and the unconfirmed cash-on-delivery line.
(4) Conversion is measured the way KZ buyers actually behave: word codes (proposed to code), a templated reply to «цена?» comments, platform DMs and comment-to-chat handoffs as tier 2, and new-contact inbound vs week 0 as the main decision read.
(5) Learning plan: week 1 publishes 4–5 bodies across 5 drivers and 5 formats (prediction_game/guess, curiosity_gap/ab_compare, identity/pov_situation, practical_value/check_before_buy, honest_signal/who_is_it_for). Each body gets 2 hook variants and is uploaded natively, as a clean master with no watermark, to TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Each platform is read against its own baseline. Per-client reads are allocation signals only; arm knowledge comes from pooling identical slugs across clients.
Known gap: the slate has no dombra, which is the most local first instrument. It is a week-0 site fetch plus one owner question, and a NEXT BATCH idea for week 2.
