# «Считаем по паспорту»: 4-week should-we-do-social test, revised (Karaganda, B2B dry mixes and insulation; Instagram + 2GIS first)

**Request:** Мы продаём только компаниям. Нам вообще нужны соцсети?
**Reframed:** «Мы продаём только компаниям. Нам вообще нужны соцсети?» → Run a cheap 4-week organic short-video test with a verdict agreed in advance: if it brings enough coded inquiries from companies per staff hour, keep going; if not, stop.
**Success metric:** Qualified coded B2B inquiries per staff hour (4-week should-we-do-social test) — target Over 4 weeks: conservative 2 / expected 5 / stretch 10 qualified coded inquiries. At about 80 planned staff minutes, that is roughly 1.5 / 3.5 / 7.5 per staff hour. Baseline: unknown, because there is no export or social account yet. The week-0 count of all inbound company inquiries (phone and WhatsApp, 2026-10-12 → 2026-10-18) becomes the baseline. Verdict threshold, set in advance: YES if at least 3 are qualified and at least 1 reaches an offer or invoice (default until the owner answers Q1). by 2026-11-16 (Each video gets its own WhatsApp code. Coded chats are counted from the weekly WhatsApp export, and phone/rep inquiries are logged as «откуда узнали?» answers. A coded inquiry counts as qualified if it names the company or ИП, the material and an approximate volume. Staff minutes come from completed shoot cards. Whether an inquiry reached an offer or invoice comes from a yes/no tag added by the rep.)

**Push back:** Честно: заранее этого не знает никто, поэтому предлагаем не спорить, а проверить за 4 недели — 0 ₸, до 20 минут в неделю у сотрудника и до 5 минут у вас. Считаем не просмотры, а обращения от компаний по коду из ролика (с названием, материалом и объёмом); порог договорим заранее, и если не наберём — прямо скажем «не нужно» и остановимся. Для B2B ролики сами по себе слабы: основные заказы всё равно идут через менеджеров и тендеры, а ролики — это витрина, которую смотрят перед первым заказом, и способ выйти на прорабов, до которых менеджеры не доходят. Тест приходится на конец сезона, поэтому «нет» в ноябре будет значить «нет на зиму», а не «нет навсегда».

## Big idea
A foreman's estimate depends on numbers already printed on the bag: consumption per m², layer thickness and how long the material keeps. A Karaganda warehouse that reads those numbers from the manufacturer's label on camera, does the bag count for his area and asks for one message («материал и площадь») is a supplier worth writing to. Episode supply: about 40 SKUs × several label lines gives more than 20 episodes. Proof on camera: the filmed label and the arithmetic done by hand on cardboard. Pointer to the measured action: «пришлите площадь — посчитаем». Every episode ends on the same local end card, so viewers and the platform read the account as a Karaganda supplier and not as generic warehouse content. Gate: no post goes out until the owner has answered the week-0 fact pack (own warehouse in Karaganda, delivery in Karaganda, 5 usually-stocked SKUs, the phone number, and whether the desk counts bags). Silence from the owner is never treated as «нет»; it blocks publishing and triggers reminders.

> Пришлите площадь — посчитаем мешки по паспорту

This is not a forecast. The honest prior for a new Instagram account in one city in November is low: probably 0–3 new-client inquiries. The plan is built so that either answer, YES or NO, can be trusted. Here is how the revision changes the plan.

**1. Each post now gives a reason to write (fixes the fatal review point).**
- The old slate showed warehouse rituals but never said what is sold, where, or what happens when you write.
- Now a fact pack is blocking in week 0: own warehouse in Karaganda, delivery inside Karaganda, 5 usually-stocked SKUs, the phone number, and whether the desk counts bags from an area. Every post ends on a fixed card: «Сухие смеси и утеплитель · склад в Караганде · пишите материал и объём». The bio, caption, hashtags and location tag carry the same line.
- Outdoor shots show local cues (gate, yard, snow).
- A NO can therefore come from the market, not from empty posts.

**2. The hero is now a service, not a lecture.** I03 (E16, E17, E19) plus I02 (E04, E25): reading the label for the foreman and counting his bags turns the message into the CTA. ID13's «вода на глаз» shortcut and its «постановка» are retired, because brigades would read them as being taught their own job. Mixing is shot at most once, as a library clip.

**3. Attribution matches how foremen actually behave.**
- One prefilled wa.me link in the bio per platform: «Здравствуйте, я из Instagram. Материал: … Площадь/объём: …».
- The phone number in large text on the end card.
- A comment keyword «расчёт» that sends an auto-DM.
- Reps ask new callers one question: «Откуда узнали?».
- Per-video codes are kept as per-video detail only.
- The verdict counts every NEW company that came from social media, by any route. This removes the strong NO bias that typed codes alone would create.

**4. The verdict cannot be pushed through by noise.**
- Only companies not already in the CRM or the reps' lists count, and at least one invoice (not just an offer) must be issued to one of them.
- Two control arms are counted separately on the same per-hour basis: rep forwarding (if approved) and a 2GIS link with its own prefill.
- The final decision is therefore 'which channel, at what cost per hour', not just 'video: yes or no'.
- A NO is worded «органика нового аккаунта за 4 недели не дала».

**5. Cost is counted honestly.**
- One sheet records every minute: cards, the voice note, the desk's labelling and replies, the reps' question, setup one-offs and the owner's minutes. The verdict is calculated per total hour.
- The truck tally (ID17) is dropped. Each chat gets one label. Weekly cards are capped at 3–4 shots in one location. The agent edits at least 2 of the 5 weekly posts from week-0 library footage.

**6. Reputation risks removed.**
- ID10 (late-truck POV) is held until delivery facts exist and the owner says «да».
- Frost is filmed only outside (gate, yard, sky), and stock only indoors.
- ID02 (wrap sound) runs once in the whole test.

**Learning plan**
- About 20 Instagram posts in the shared driver and format slugs, pooled into the b2b_construction_materials vertical.
- TikTok and Shorts are added only if Instagram shows life by the end of week 2.
- Instagram audience-city share is tracked, to check the reach is actually local.

**METHOD gaps (judgment)**
- F3 has no mix for should_we_do_social. Used: about 50% hero utility, 30% conversion questions, 20% reach and moment probes.
- Standing owner approval with silent consent is used only if the owner opts in once (L8 is kept as a one-time human decision).

## Measurement and decisions
- Metric: New qualified company inquiries from social media per TOTAL hour over the test (posts 2026-10-26 → 11-22), plus at least one invoice issued to a new client.
- **New:** not in the CRM or the reps' lists, checked by the rep in week 4.
- **Qualified:** names the company or ИП, the material and an area or volume. Partial inquiries are reported separately; private buyers never count.
- **Source = social** by ANY route: a per-video code, the bio prefill, the «расчёт» keyword DM, or a rep's «Откуда узнали?» answer «соцсети».
- **Total hour:** every minute in the total-time sheet, covering cards, the voice note, desk labels and replies, the rep question, setup one-offs and the owner. Estimated at about 130 min including about 70 min of week-0 setup.
- **Target:** conservative 2 / expected 5 / stretch 10 new qualified companies, which is about 0.9 / 2.3 / 4.6 per total hour.
- **Baseline:** the rep's retro count of new companies invoiced per week in August–September.
