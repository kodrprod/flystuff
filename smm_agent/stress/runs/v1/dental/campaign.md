## Big idea
People put off implants because the answers they need come from a surgeon they haven't met yet: does it hurt, what does the price depend on, will they push me into treatment. In this plan the clinic's implant surgeon answers those questions personally. He answers patients already in the clinic's records directly, which gets bookings in October. He answers everyone else on camera with recurring hidden implant steps: the real implant, the plan on the jaw model, and the cases where he advises against one. This can produce 20+ episodes, because each patient question and each implant-specific step is its own episode. It is provable on camera, because only surgeon-confirmed steps and words appear. It points at the measured action, a coded booking or a reply of «ВРАЧ».

> Сначала спросите хирурга — решите потом

This is not a forecast. Every insight is PROVISIONAL (strength 2): the client is hypothetical and the ledger is empty. The message only invites people to ask; nothing is claimed about the business. It becomes fully true once the surgeon confirms two things in the week-0 session: that the consultation carries no obligation (NEEDS FACT, I02/E17) and that he answers patient questions.

What changed after review, and why:

(1) The base is now the main lever from week 0. Patients already in the clinic's records (MIS) get a personal, doctor-approved WhatsApp message in waves sized to capacity, with reply keyword «ВРАЧ». This is the fastest route to October bookings. Organic content alone has a 10–20× uncertainty range (REALISM in the intake). The base is measured as its own arm so it doesn't hide what content does.

(2) The hero is now «Вопрос хирургу», built on I01, I02 and I03 (E13, E17, E20, E10, E23). It answers fear, the sales-trap worry and price. Sterilisation is no longer the hero. It is one episode (ID02), because it is a worn category convention and not specific to implants.

(3) Price is addressed in week 1. A «от чего зависит цена импланта» episode (I03, no figures) is fast-tracked through step 4. Prices or an «от» range appear only after the owner sends a dated price list.

(4) The CTAs promise something worth writing for: the surgeon's preliminary view of the likely stages, from a photo or X-ray. This is a non-price offer that needs owner and surgeon approval, plus a daily cap.

(5) The surgeon's face is the default ask, because for a surgery decision people want to see who they are going to. A recurring name tag is the fallback. This also tests the shared experiment, face vs hands in frame 1.

(6) Compliance gates: RU+KZ hook and CTA on every video, checked by a native speaker; licence number in the bio and caption; the sanitary-regime lead checks any sterilisation frame. ID20 (patient voice) is removed.

Learning plan: each post is read at 48 h and 7 days against the pooled dental median until the account has 10 posts of its own. Allocation runs on relative views plus shares and saves until 20 coded chats, then on coded chats per 1k views. The base arm is read separately as replies and bookings per message sent.

## Measurement and decisions
- Metric: Booked implant consultations, coded (video code or «ВРАЧ», via WhatsApp / Instagram DM / phone), matched to the admin label «Имплант-консультация» and checked for attendance in the schedule. Target: conservative 1.2× / expected 1.5× / stretch 2× the weekly baseline, averaged over weeks 3–4, by 2026-11-15. Baseline = mean weekly implant consultations from the MIS, 2026-09-14 → 10-11. If unavailable, use the week-0 count of uncoded bookings. Absolute numbers and the REALISM verdict are fixed on 2026-11-01. Reported in three lines: base arm («ВРАЧ»), content-coded (video codes, a floor), and total vs baseline (estimate). Lagging metric: plans accepted, to 2026-12-31. REALISM update: [ASSUMPTION] reply rate 5–15% × booking 30–50% → ~2–8 consultations per 100 base messages. The list size is unknown, so the owner's «сотни» is unverified; the estimate is replaced by wave-1 measured rates.

The base arm uses the keyword «ВРАЧ», counted separately and never mixed into content results. Phone: the admin asks for and logs the code (judgment extension). Instagram DMs labelled the same way. Codeless chats are asked «Откуда о нас узнали?». The 30-day attribution window covers multi-week decisions. Paid traffic (2GIS/boost) gets its own codes and is reported as its own line.
