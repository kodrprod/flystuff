## Big idea
An online buyer can't judge an instrument from a spec sheet or a price tag. So we play regularly stocked instruments up close in the Astana store: hands only, the phone near the strings, keys or speaker, recorded in a quiet hour. Where a second model is in stock, we play the two side by side so the buyer can hear the difference and decide by ear. We also state plainly what the whole start costs and what is and isn't in the box. Each episode ends with one word (ГИТАРА / ПИАНИНО / УКУЛЕЛЕ) that the viewer can send in WhatsApp, Direct or a comment to ask about that model. The in-stock catalogue supports 20+ episodes. Each one is proven by sound on camera, and each points at a countable inquiry.

> Послушайте, прежде чем платить

This is a reasoned bet and a learning plan, not a forecast (L1).
(1) It sits on the strongest tension. I01 (strength 4; E01, E02, E12–E17, E50): buyers fear a cheap first guitar is a toy and can't hear that from a catalogue. ID01 answers this with the real sound of the AS-100BK (as100_price, 59 800 ₸, 11 in stock per the site sample, re-fetched on publish day).
