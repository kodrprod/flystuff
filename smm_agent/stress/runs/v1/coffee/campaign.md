# Left Bank launch v2 — «Узнаёте это место?» (the neighbours' view, one word for a reminder, a week-long opening ritual)

**Request:** Через 3 недели открываем вторую точку на Левом берегу. Нужен ажиотаж.
**Reframed:** «Через 3 недели открываем вторую точку на Левом берегу. Нужен ажиотаж.» → Before the second location opens, build a list of nearby people who ask to be told when and where it opens, using a WhatsApp code from each video. Then turn that list, plus the first location's regulars, into steady paid visits at the Левый берег location over its first 4 weeks. A one-day queue for photos is not the goal.
**Success metric:** Coded opening sign-ups for the new location (people who send a video's code to get the opening date and address) — target conservative 40 / expected 80 / stretch 150 coded sign-ups. These are assumptions: no baseline exists. The target is re-fixed on 2026-10-16 from week-1 coded chats per 1k views. Baseline: 0 (a new location with no list). by 2026-10-30 (Messages that contain a per-video code ({CODE}), counted from the weekly WhatsApp chat export or Instagram DMs (whichever channel Q3 picks) and deduplicated by contact. Supporting directional count: Левый берег POS checks for days 1–14, plus a barista tick sheet asking «Откуда узнали?» (1 tap per guest, opening week only).)

**Push back:** Гарантировать ажиотаж за 3 недели не может никто: охват коротких видео непредсказуем, а алгоритму нужно время, чтобы найти людей именно с Левого берега. Поэтому успех считаем не в просмотрах. Считаем, сколько людей написали код из ролика, чтобы узнать дату и адрес, и сколько чеков будет на новой точке в первые 4 недели. По расчёту одной органики, скорее всего, не хватит, поэтому главные рычаги — ваши постоянные гости (бариста на первой точке, Статус WhatsApp), карточка новой точки в 2ГИС до открытия и, если решите, недорогое продвижение лучшего ролика вокруг нового адреса; дату, адрес и акции в роликах не называем, пока вы их не подтвердите.

## Big idea
People on Левый берег learn about the new coffee shop the way a neighbour would. They see the view from its window (their own street, courtyard or bus stop), the room behind the glass, the people who will make their morning coffee, and an opening week they can take part in. The address and landmark are public, the same as on the 2GIS card. Viewers comment or DM one word, «ЛЕВЫЙ», to get a reminder on opening day. Generative: 20+ episodes come from the window view across the fit-out, real neighbour questions, the regulars' preview and the opening-week ritual. Provable: everything is filmed on site. Measurable: the keyword per post, the codes on the poster, counter card and Status, and POS checks from opening day. Truth gate: nothing runs until the owner confirms in writing the location, the landmark or address we may name, and permission to film the new site (E02, E21).

> Узнаёте это место? Скоро здесь кофе

The ledger is empty, so the message cites no fact ids yet. It is true by construction once the owner confirms the location and that the site may be filmed (E02, E21 → NEEDS FACT). Every insight is strength ≤2, so this is a learning bet, not a forecast (L001).

What changed from v1, and why:
(1) Location is the thing people share. The landmark or address now goes in the hook and on screen of every episode, wherever the owner allows it (same data as the 2GIS card). This fixes the contradiction in v1, which hid the address in videos while publishing it on 2GIS. A forwarded line like «смотри, это же у нас во дворе» only works if the place is recognisable. I03 (moment: a closed shopfront on the daily route, E21/E18/E11/E23) is re-dramatised as the view from the window, not an empty-room-plus-tamper cliché.
(2) The measured action is now one tap: comment or DM «ЛЕВЫЙ», and we remind you on opening day. In v1 people had to DM a code to get an address, which nobody does (I02 friction, E02/E03/E15/E26/E09/E25). The keyword also opts people in to the opening-day message.
(3) Most of the volume comes from channels outside Reels, because the REALISM ratio (≈1–33 organic) says a ~2.1k account built around location 1 cannot reach Левый берег alone. These are: a window poster on the covered shopfront, 2–3 nearby building chats posted by the owner, a geo-boost decided now (not on 19.10), the location-1 counter card and a regulars' preview evening (I04/I05 warm base, E05/E14/E16/E22).
(4) «Ажиотаж» gets a real reason to come: an opening-week ritual with the owner's offer, redeemed at the till with the same word. Visits spread naturally across a week-long ritual, so nobody is told not to come. ID06 is dropped, and capacity is handled by staffing.
(5) Staff load is cut to ≤10 min/week of filming. The ID19 tally, voice notes and opening-week tick sheet are gone, and one named person answers messages behind an instant auto-reply.

Learning plan: 5 posts in week 1 across 5 drivers (curiosity_gap, identity, amusement_awe, story, relief). Each is read at 48 h internally, and the target is re-fixed on 16.10. The owner sees only a 3-number Monday report; allocation statistics stay inside code. JUDGMENT: F6 assumes a 4-week runway, but here there are 3 weeks before opening, so reach and conversion run together from week 1.
